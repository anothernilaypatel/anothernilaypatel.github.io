Thirteen hours. A beginner might need 50 to 75, and I have not studied finance the way someone earning a degree in it has. I have worked in the field, at Jaguar Health and at Golden Egg Labs, and that was not close to enough. I gave myself two weeks, and I still had a full workload.

## The one exam you can take alone

The SIE is the mandatory first exam for working with a firm's finances. It is the gateway from studying the subject to doing the job. Later qualification exams only open if the firm sponsors you. The SIE is the exception, so I took it on my own.

## Seven files, one hour each

[CertFuel](https://www.certfuel.com/sie/) has free SIE material. The lessons are generated with AI, and the videos are hard to sit through, so I downloaded the transcripts and had Cursor turn the units into summarized Markdown files. Markdown is what makes the loop fast. A model can reread a file, tighten it, and carry that version into the next pass.

Seven files, each one a day's work. I spent an hour on each, then opened them in [Markdown Live Preview](https://markdownlivepreview.com/) and studied from the rendered page.

![Seven Markdown notes, one file per study day](./passing-the-sie/seven-files.png "Seven files, from capital markets through the regulatory framework.")

The first practice exam took 30 minutes. I failed it. The notes needed another pass.

## A page, and a timer

I learn faster when I can see the material, and a Markdown file will take a diagram without a fuss. I was also out of days. I had been driving up and down I-95 to see family. I asked for the notes on one page, with the important lines highlighted, and a timer so I would not linger on a section.

Two more practice exams after that. Both of them stopped one answer short of a pass.

> Fifty-two correct. Fifty-three would have passed.

![A practice score of 69 percent, 52 of 75](./passing-the-sie/fifty-two.png "52 of 75, under the 70 percent line.")

## Eleven and a half hours in

I had hoped to be further along. Life caught up. Each unit was coming back with a similar score, so the last hour did not need to be a reread of everything. It needed to go after the points still available.

The morning of the exam I finished that one-hour review and took a final practice exam. I passed it. Then I had to go take the real one.

![A study plan ranked by points still available](./passing-the-sie/study-plan.png "The morning-of review, ordered by how many points a unit could still add.")

## The full 105 minutes

Every practice exam, I finished in about 30 minutes and answered on the first instinct. The real exam allows 105. A few questions did stop me. With the rest of the time I could reason through them and pick the better of the answers left.

I left more sure of that sitting than of any practice exam. I will never know the score. It was enough to pass.

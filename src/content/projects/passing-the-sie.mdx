The SIE Exam is a fundamental, mandatory exam in order to work with the financials for deemed companies. It is the first gateway exam that turns finance students into working in the corporate world. It is usually sponsored by the firm hiring them, and the further qualification exams are only possible to take if a firm sponsors them. The SIE is the one exception to this rule, so I decided to embark on taking this exam.

There is one slight problem: I have not studied finance, at least not nearly enough as someone getting an undergraduate degree in it. I have had some experience working in the finance sector for Jaguar Health and Golden Egg Labs, but that is not nearly enough. To add insult to injury, I gave myself two functional weeks to pass this exam, all while maintaining a steady work courseload. As a functional beginner, it might take them 50 to 75 hours to study for this exam: I passed it with 13.

As AI has become more powerful, I decided to have it guide me on this process. To start with, [CertFuel](https://www.certfuel.com/sie/) has free SIE materials to help people study. Their content is generated with AI, so their videos are a bit hard to digest, so I decided to download their transcripts, feed them through Cursor, and have it split up the key units into summarized Markdown files for notes. Markdown files are crucial to leveling up the efficiency of AI since AI is very good at managing Markdown files. You can use Markdown files to store information to feed into future models to make them more specialized as well as store information so that models can crystallize that information through multiple iterations.

After getting 7 markdown files (each of them being spliced into a day's worth of studying), I spent an hour going through each of them to learn that material. That gave me a great guideline for the underlying material. I opened these markdown files in [Markdown Live Preview](https://markdownlivepreview.com/) and used the preview as a study guide. After taking a practice exam in 30 minutes, I failed, and realized that I needed to re-study.

![Seven Markdown notes, one file per study day](./passing-the-sie/seven-files.png "Seven files, from capital markets through the regulatory framework.")

I realized that I am a visual learner, and the nice thing about markdown files is that implementing visuals is seamless. I also was starting to run out of time, since I have been driving constantly up and down I-95 to see family. So, I told the AI to compile these notes, litter it with visuals, make it color coded/highlighted, and to slam a timer on [the website](/sie-study/) to make sure I don't spend too long on this.

After studying for this, I took two more practice exams, both on the brink of passing. I had to call it for the night, but it is a bit frustrating scoring 52 questions correct when 53 questions would be a passing score.

![A practice score of 69 percent, 52 of 75](./passing-the-sie/fifty-two.png "52 of 75, under the 70 percent line.")

At this point, I have studied for 11 and a half hours. I was hoping to have more studying done by then, but life caught up to me, and I needed to figure out how to pass this exam consistently. However, there is one redeeming trait of this. I was constantly scoring a similar number of points for each unit. In a final sprint, I had AI create a targeted, 1 hour review to optimize for points in this area. So, the morning of the exam, I committed to finishing this review and taking a final exam.

![A study plan ranked by points still available](./passing-the-sie/study-plan.png "The morning-of review, ordered by how many points a unit could still add.")

After the targeted review and taking the exam, I passed my first practice exam. At this point in time, I must now get ready to go take the real exam.

While taking the exam, I realized one thing that I have not been accounting for. For each of my practice exams, I would only complete it in 30 minutes, and only answer off of my first intuition. Even though the exam had a few questions that truly stumped me, when you use the full allotted 105 minutes, that gives you time to reason through each question to pick the most reasonable answer. After submitting the exam, I felt way more comfortable with that exam compared to all of the practice exam. I will never know what I truly scored on this exam, but it clearly was enough for me to pass!

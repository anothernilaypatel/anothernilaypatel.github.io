The prices are what the lab paid in 2019 or 2022. ConsumerLab.com is the source for the tests and the purchase prices. Every bar from the lab test is on [the surface](/chocolate-surface/), not only the Pareto bars.

Height is flavanols, in milligrams per gram. Lead and cadmium are the other two axes, in micrograms per gram. Larger dots deliver more flavanols per dollar. Drag to rotate. `?bare=1` leaves the plot alone.

A bar is a Pareto bar when no other bar has at least as many flavanols per gram and no more lead or cadmium per gram, and is strictly better on one of the three. The Pareto bars are Ghirardelli 72%, Montezuma's 100%, Baker's 56%, Lily's 70%, and ChocZero 70%.

Bang for the buck is a separate score. The value score multiplies the dollars for 200 mg of flavanols by the metal score. Lower is better. The metal score divides the lead in that 200 mg by 0.5 µg, then adds the cadmium in that 200 mg divided by 4.1 µg. A lead value marked with a less-than sign is the lab ceiling, and the value score gets that same mark.

Ghirardelli 72% is the best value: $0.53 for 200 mg of flavanols, with the value score marked under 0.37. Its lead number is a lab ceiling.
